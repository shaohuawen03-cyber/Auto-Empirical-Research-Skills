# Journal targeting notes / 选刊分析（v0.5）

Final selection belongs to the author. Analysis below assumes the manuscript stays a **computational triage + structure-based hypothesis-generation paper** (no wet data).

## Primary candidates / 首选方向（computational / bioinformatics）

| Journal | Fit | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal* | screening + docking + honest MD-failure reporting fits scope | check word/figure limits; methods transparency expectations match |
| *Briefings in Bioinformatics* | UniDL4BioPep-stack methodology; but usually review-leaning for methods | confirm article type accepts original screening studies |
| *Journal of Alzheimer’s Disease* | disease framing, microbiome section exists | docking-only structure stage may be light for main section |
| *Scientific Reports* | broad, methods-tolerant, accepts negative-boundary framing | volume; fees; figure spec |

## Cross-repo precedent (Light-skills) / 姊妹仓库先例

The Light-skills branch suggested **BMC Oral Health** (first choice) and **Scientific Reports** (broad backup)
for the sequence-less v2 package. With sequences and docking now included, the oral-health fit improves
(periodontitis angle), but the paper is now more structure-heavy; a CSBJ-type venue may review it faster.
Recommend the author send the pre-submission enquiry to **two** venues (one oral-health, one computational).

## Recheck before submission / 投稿前复查

Scope, article type, indexing, APC, word limits, reference style, figure specifications, AI-use policy —
each must be re-read on the journal’s site; nothing here substitutes for the official instructions.
