# Highlights (bilingual) / 亮点（双语）

## English

- Proteomes discard >99.7% of translated oral sORFs before any AD filter (11.7 M → 33,786).
- BBB + neurotoxicity + metal + chelator-high/scavenger-low stack leaves 12 (strict: 8) candidates.
- PAS-centred AChE docking yields a descriptive 12-peptide ranking (Vina −8.25 to −9.60 kcal/mol), not affinity.
- His/Cys-rich cationic composition provides experimentally testable metal-coordination handles.
- MD attempted, reported as excluded; wet assays remain the stopping rule — no mechanism is claimed.
