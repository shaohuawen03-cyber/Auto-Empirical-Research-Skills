Date: 2026-08-12 (v0.5).

## Search axes / 检索轴

1. **AD × AChE non-canonical function**: PAS-driven Aβ fibril acceleration (Inestrosa 1996; De Ferrari 2001; Bartolini 2003), gorge structures (Kryger 1999; Cheung 2012/PDB 4EY6), Aβ residence patch 344–361 (Atanasova 2020; Lushchekina 2017).
2. **Periodontitis × AD**: epidemiology (Ide 2016; Sparks Stein 2007; Jiang 2021), gingipains/OMV (Dominy 2019; Poole 2013; Ilievski 2018; Haditsch 2020; Ho 2015; Nara 2021; Guo 2010; Ryder 2020; Kanagasingam 2020; Díaz-Zúñiga 2020; flagged Frontiers 2026 clinical record).
3. **Metal hypothesis**: Bush 2013; Lei/Ayton/Bush 2021.
4. **Exogenous toxic-peptide template**（作者提供的文献框架，逐条核验）: Di Natale 2018 (tau26–44 + Cu(II)); Perini 2019 (tau26–44 dynamics); Lei 2021 (elementomics); Chen 2016 (curli cross-seeding).
5. **sORF/micropeptide biology**: Couso & Patra 2017; van Heesch 2019.
6. **Methods**: Vina (Trott & Olson 2010; Eberhardt 2021), FlexPepDock (London 2011), AlphaFold3 (Abramson 2024), GROMACS stack (Abraham 2015; Hornak 2006; Jorgensen 1983; Berendsen 1984), MM/GBSA (Genheden & Ryde 2015), UniDL4BioPep (Du 2023).

## Novelty statement / 查新结论

No located study combines: (i) a proteome-supported periodontitis sORF screen with BBB/neurotoxicity/metal
stacking, (ii) printed candidate sequences, and (iii) PAS-focused docking against human AChE. The closest
structure-level work is Aβ itself on AChE (Atanasova 2020). Coverage is bounded: no paid-database sweep was
performed; the flagged 2026 Frontiers record awaits author-side verification.
