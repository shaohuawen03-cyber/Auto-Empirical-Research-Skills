# Stage 1 — Topic and locked claim / 选题与锁定论断

Date: 2026-08-12 (v0.5).

## Locked claim / 锁定论断

A periodontitis-associated, proteome-supported sORF screen can be reduced to a 12-peptide (8-peptide)
computational shortlist by stacking BBB, neurotoxicity, metal-binding and chelator-high / scavenger-low
filters, and all twelve candidates can occupy the PAS–gorge interface of human AChE (4EY6) in
PAS-focused AutoDock Vina docking.

## Not the claim / 非论断

1. The twelve peptides are *P. gingivalis*-derived (taxonomy unassigned; community metagenome).
2. The peptides generate ROS, bind metals experimentally, or inhibit/chaperone AChE activity.
3. Vina scores are binding constants.
4. Periodontitis peptidomes are more BBB-positive by rate (only the long branch rate moves; short-branch rates are flat).
5. Any causal statement about periodontitis and AD.
