# Research ethics review / 科研伦理审查（v0.5）

Date: 2026-08-12.

## Human subjects / 人体受试

No new human sampling. The screen re-analyses public, de-identified assemblies (PRJNA678453 / PRJEB65451)
and public proteomes (PXD003151, PXD004319, PXD026727, HOMD). The original cohort consent belongs to the
depositors (Belstrøm et al. 2021, ref 33). Authors must confirm that the reuse falls within the deposited
consent scope before submission.

## Animal work / 动物实验

None in this study. Animal work cited as background (Dominy 2019; Ilievski 2018; Díaz-Zúñiga 2020; Chen 2016)
was performed by others under their own approvals.

## Data provenance / 数据来源

All screening numbers trace to the author’s draft; docking numbers trace to the author’s docking note.
No third-party non-public data were used. Public identifiers are listed in Data Availability.

## Misrepresentation safeguards / 防误传

- GSE42872 melanoma materials are excluded from the evidence base (`../evidence/excluded_source_record.md`).
- Two erroneous literature identifiers supplied by the author were corrected against PubMed/PDB rather than propagated.
- AI-assistance disclosure is included in the Declarations.

## Open ethics items for the author / 待作者完成的伦理项

Ethics approval/exemption statement wording; consent statement confirmation; AI-use disclosure final text;
competing-interest and funding declarations.
